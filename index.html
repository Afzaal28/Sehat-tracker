Why lift, not just eat

To gain healthy weight (shape, not just fat) the extra calories need somewhere to go. Strength training 3–4 evenings a week turns food into tone. For "abs": there's no spot-reduction — core work builds and strengthens the muscles, and at her lean weight they show as that strength grows. Keep cardio light so it doesn't burn the surplus. No gym needed — water bottles work as light weights.

Rest 60–90 sec between sets. Best done after work, ~1 hour before dinner. Start light with clean form, add a little each week. Rest days are when muscle actually grows.
